About

Three years, one recurring job: making systems agree.

Every project on this page has the same shape at its core — two systems that need to share data reliably, in real time, without a human in the loop. Whether that's a Jira board, a WhatsApp thread, a Google Shared Drive or a SharePoint folder, the job is the same: build the Apex, the triggers, and the validation that make the handoff invisible.

That's meant going deep on Apex triggers and bulkification, Lightning Web Components, Visualforce, and the REST layer that ties Salesforce to the outside world — always with test coverage above 90%, because integrations fail quietly if you let them.
